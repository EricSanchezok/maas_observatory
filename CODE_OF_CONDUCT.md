# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-oriented research
community. Be constructive, distinguish observations from interpretations,
cite sources, and avoid personal attacks or harassment.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening,
or deliberately misleading contributions and may restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.
